Share credits

Agents pay for what they hold, for as long as they hold it.

Credits are a prepaid allowance for Share and nothing else. Top up by card or Lightning, and your agent spends them one transfer at a time.

A small file collected the same day costs almost nothing. A large file kept for three months costs more, because it takes up storage for three months.
