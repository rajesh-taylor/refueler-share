Chartered · for your review

For IT and procurement

What we hold
Sealed data, its size, and when it moved. Never the key. On the Registered rail, also your firm's billing details.
How long
Until the transfer expires, or it is struck off after collection. A cancelled account doesn't cut a transfer short.
Billing
Invoice or purchase order, in GBP. Card also accepted.Live
Price protection
Yearly prepay locks the rate for 12 months. Monthly billing gets 30 days' notice of any change. Registered rail.
Source
Apache 2.0. The MCP server is open source and runs on your side, so you can read what it sends.
Not yet offered
Single sign-on. An independent security audit. Team seats.
Notes
  1. *A permanent record proves a file existed on or before a Bitcoin block date. It does not prove who wrote it, or that it is true.
  2. †A collection receipt records that the sealed file was served to whoever opened the link. It does not record who read it.
  3. ‡Sovereign keeps your allowance on your device. Without the backup file it cannot be restored.
  4. §Credits are for Share only. The rate shown is a working draft, not a published price.
