A personal key lets your assistant lodge files from your Citizen or Sovereign allowance, within a daily limit you set.

Notes to the register
  1. *A permanent record proves a file existed on or before a Bitcoin block date. It does not prove who wrote it, or that it is true.
  2. †A collection receipt records that the sealed file was served to whoever opened the link. It does not record who read it.
  3. ‡Sovereign keeps your allowance on your device. Without the backup file it cannot be restored.
  4. §Credits are for Share only. The rate shown is a working draft, not a published price.
