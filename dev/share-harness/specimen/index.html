Motion · four verbs, one easing

Arrive, settle, fade the changed figure, press. One easing cubic-bezier(.2, .7, .2, 1). Opacity and a 6 px rise only — nothing scales, bounces, slides in from the side or moves on its own. “Reduced motion” at the top (or the OS setting) turns every one of them off. Doc §7.

Haptics · proposed off in v1

Safari (Mac and iPhone) has no vibration for web pages at all. Android browsers built on Chrome (Vanadium included) can give one short tick, only right after a tap. iPhone gives its own tick when a native switch is toggled (iOS 18). To feel these on a phone, open http://192.168.0.199:8093 on the same Wi-Fi (Mullvad: turn on “Local network sharing”). Doc §8.

The copy tick · 10 ms, once

How this link works

Graded dial · Carbon only

Use “Dial 1 %” / “Dial 2 %” at the top: the whole page lifts very slightly toward the centre, like a Polaris dial. Look for rings (banding) on the grey, on the Mac, the iPhone and any ordinary monitor; “Dither” adds a fine grain that should hide them. If you notice the dial as a spotlight, it’s too much. Decided 13 Oct: Carbon Dial 1 % with dither is the default (and this page’s default); Paper stays flat; a month of alpha feedback, then revisit. Doc §12.
